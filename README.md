### GenAI security, in the open.

I'm Emmanuel — a GenAI security researcher and contributor to the [OWASP GenAI Security Project](https://genai.owasp.org/), based in Canada. My work focuses on making emerging AI risks legible to security teams: mapping LLM, agentic, and data security threats to the frameworks they already operate on (NIST AI RMF, ISO/IEC 42001, EU AI Act, MITRE ATLAS).

Most of what I do is open source. The pinned repos below are where I'm putting the time.

---

### Featured projects

**[GenAI-Security-Crosswalk](https://github.com/emmanuelgjr/GenAI-Security-Crosswalk)**
The most comprehensive open-source mapping of OWASP GenAI risks to 16 industry frameworks. Covers the LLM Top 10, Agentic Top 10, and DSGAI 2026 across 37 files — including OT/ICS, EU AI Act, NIST, and ISO.

**[DSGAI](https://github.com/emmanuelgjr/DSGAI)**
Interactive web guide for OWASP's *GenAI Data Security Risks and Mitigations 2026* — 21 risk entries, navigable.

**[2025 LLM Top 10 Mapping](https://github.com/emmanuelgjr/2025LLMTop10Mapping)**
The OWASP Top 10 for LLM & GenAI Apps 2025, mapped to broader cybersecurity frameworks.

**[AgentVulnMitigator](https://github.com/emmanuelgjr/AgentVulnMitigator)**
A general-purpose multi-agent tool for detecting and mitigating vulnerabilities in agentic AI systems.
